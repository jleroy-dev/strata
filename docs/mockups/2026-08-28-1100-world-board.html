One world · how much curvature, and where a repo stands on it

Every card draws the same thing: all 60 git repos in ~/QuartzX, laid out by core's own placeBlocks and layoutOf as they are today, district rects and tower cells included, then shelved into one ground. A continent is a repo, a city is a project, a district is a folder, a block is a tower. Nothing is invented and nothing is sampled. The only variable in cards A to D is the radius of the single sphere the flat lattice is bent over; card E changes where continents stand; card F lights it. Drag any card to orbit. Only card F moves on its own.
