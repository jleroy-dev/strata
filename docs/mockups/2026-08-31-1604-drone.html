The drone · free flight over the world

The terrain is the real one: strata and the dev scratch, exactly as a running server places them, countries, folders and tower heights included. Plate skirts, slab heights and colours come from terrain.ts and theme.ts, and the ground is bent over the world sphere at WORLD_RADIUS, so what you fly through is the map and not a stand-in. The two agents are invented: they exist so that soft lock has something to lock onto. Click the view, then fly: WASD to move, E and Q for altitude, Shift to boost, Ctrl for precision, Hold Space to fire the striker, which rings whatever it lands on without changing it, and F to fire the grapple, which bites a tower and reels you to it. A hit is pitched by the height of the tower, so a big file answers lower than a small one and a district has a chord. T to lock the nearest agent, Esc to leave. 1 to 4 jump to the four vantages the board is meant to be judged from: the world, a skyline down packages/core, a worksite inside packages/web, and the water between the two repos. Both movement schemes are on the toggle; everything else on the right is an open number, and the point of the board is to settle them by flying rather than on paper.
