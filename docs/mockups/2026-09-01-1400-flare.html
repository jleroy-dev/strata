Read off the Star Fox 64 reference rather than invented: a long tapered sliver rather than a stubby one, a four pointed star at the muzzle and at the hit rather than a round blob, and glows that are large and diffuse rather than small and hot. The towers stand in for scale at the proportions terrain.ts draws, and every card runs the panel's own bloom at the theme.ts values. The reference's camera sits behind and above its ship, so its bolts are seen three quarter on; ours sits directly behind, which is why the muzzle spread slider matters more than any shape here.
