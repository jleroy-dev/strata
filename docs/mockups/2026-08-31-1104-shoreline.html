The shore · how much land a continent shows around its countries

Every card draws the same two repos: strata and the dev scratch, exactly as the layout on a running server places them today, countries, folders and tower heights included. Nothing is invented and nothing is sampled. The only variable is how far the land reaches past the countries standing on it, and in cards E and F whether the minimum plate still applies to the land. Plate skirts, slab heights and colours are the real ones from terrain.ts and theme.ts. Rows one and three frame the strata island; the scratch island moves between cards because a wider shore can step the claim ladder and reshelve the world, which is the reservation doing its job. Drag any card to orbit.
