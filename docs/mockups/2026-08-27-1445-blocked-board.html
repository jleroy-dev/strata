Every card shows the same three beacons at the same scale: thinking (a prompt landed or a tool finished, the agent is working on something the panel cannot name), waiting (the turn is over), and blocked (a permission or a question is in the way). Thinking and waiting are settled and identical in all four cards: thinking keeps the live halo and the quick breathe, waiting drops to half and slows. Only the treatment of blocked changes. None of the candidates adds a fourth kind of motion, so the read/edit/run trio keeps its three exclusive axes.

The test is peripheral, not central: glance away from the screen and back, and see whether the blocked one is the one your eye lands on.
